# FRD-08 — Help, contact and legal

## Overview

Help, contact and legal covers help, contact and legal. It is specified as its own feature so it can be built, reviewed and released independently.

## Requirements

### REQ-08-001 — Help and FAQ pages

- AC-08-001.1 — WHEN the owner opens the screen, the system SHALL show help and FAQ pages.
- AC-08-001.2 — IF the source is missing, THEN the system SHALL show the designed empty state.

### REQ-08-002 — Contact form and legal pages

- AC-08-002.1 — WHEN the owner opens the screen, the system SHALL show contact form and legal pages.
- AC-08-002.2 — IF the source is missing, THEN the system SHALL show the designed empty state.

## Out of scope

- Anything that writes to the factory or calls an external service.
