# FRD-08 — Documentation

The **"Documentación"** surface: the navigable face of the factory's know-how inside Pandacorp, to understand the system (including the handoff to another person). It is the **operable surface of the factory's know-how**, organized with the **Diátaxis** model and surfaced as the sidebar destination labelled **"Documentación"** (the contract name — it is NOT called "Manual" or "Códice"; "el códice del gremio" may appear only as flavor copy in the subtitle).

> **Design freeze amendment (2026-10-22, S2.2).** The approved Órbita prototype is the oracle. The previously unnumbered criteria are now numbered REQ-08-001 to REQ-08-019 in file order (the old `FRD-08#Uk` names map to REQ-08-0kk). Items the owner left out of v2 (decision 2026-10-21) stay in place, prefixed **Out of v2**. The multi-runtime criteria follow DR-133 (owner decision 2026-10-20), which supersedes DR-120. Prototype-drawn behaviours are REQ-08-020 (AC-08-020.1 to AC-08-020.3). Source: `docs/design/freeze/reconciliation.md`, `docs/design/freeze/copy/frd-08.md`.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
- **REQ-08-001** — The Documentation SHALL be labelled **"Documentación"** in the sidebar and as its page H1, and every interface string that names the surface (search labels, placeholders, index labels, the not-found page: "La Documentación no tiene una página «no-existe».", "Ir al inicio de la Documentación") SHALL say "Documentación"; "Manual" / "Códice" SHALL NOT be the surface name. The short rail and bottom-navigation label SHALL be "Docs" (or "Documentación" where it fits at 12 px).
- **REQ-08-002** — The Documentation SHALL offer a reading area that renders each page and a page index to the RIGHT of it (AC-08-020.3), folded below 900 px.
- **REQ-08-003** — The pages SHALL be organized in four groups: **Tutorial** ("Empieza aquí" — learning-oriented, the first-mission walkthrough), **Guías** (task-oriented), **Conceptos** (understanding-oriented, the why) and **Workflows**, plus a **Referencia** entry that points to Configuración (FRD-07), where the catalogs live. Every documentation page SHALL belong to one of the four groups.
- **REQ-08-004** — The pages SHALL cover, at minimum: what Pandacorp is, the flow, the stages in detail, the commands, the implementation modes, the standards, and how to operate / hand off to another person.
- **REQ-08-005** — The content SHALL be sufficient for someone with no prior context to understand how the factory works and how to operate it.
- **REQ-08-006** — The catalogs behind Referencia (Configuración: commands (skills), agents (party), decision rules, and standards) SHALL be **derived dynamically from the factory at build/read time** from the canonical source: the plugin skill and agent frontmatter (name + description) under `plugin/skills/` and `plugin/agents/`, `factory/decisions/registry.yaml`, and `factory/standards/`. They SHALL NOT be a hand-maintained copy, so that a **new, renamed or removed** skill, agent, rule or standard appears there **automatically** with no drift (DR-046).
- **REQ-08-007** — Referencia SHALL point to the Configuración (FRD-07) surfaces rather than re-implement them, so there is one catalog, one set of cards and one detail per item.
- **REQ-08-008** — **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** the **skill detail** SHALL present a **curated, plain-language explainer** (Spanish, "for-dummies") of what the skill does, when it is used and why it matters — from a hand-authored layer, NOT the raw English frontmatter description — falling back to the frontmatter description only when no explainer is authored.
- **REQ-08-009** — **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** the skill detail SHALL render an **interactive, step-by-step flow graph**: ordered, **typed** steps (a normal step, a human-gate/decision, a safe point, an owner↔IA exchange, a repeating phase — each colour-coded by type), with parallel steps and the repeating loop made explicit, so a reader with no prior context can follow how the skill works.
- **REQ-08-010** — **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** WHERE a flow step invokes another skill or an agent, that node SHALL be **clickable cross-navigation**: clicking a skill node SHALL open that skill's own doc (with its own flow graph); clicking an agent node SHALL open that agent's card — so the Documentation is a **navigable graph**, not a set of flat pages.
- **REQ-08-011** — The full **`SKILL.md`** SHALL remain available, rendered as **markdown** behind "Definición completa" in the Configuración skill (and agent) dialog, NOT a raw unformatted text dump.
- **REQ-08-012** — **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** the curated explainer + flow layer SHALL be **hand-authored** (`src/lib/manual/skill-flows.ts`) and SHALL reflect the **real** `plugin/skills/<slug>/SKILL.md` body + the factory decision rules (accuracy contract); when a skill's flow changes, its authored flow SHALL be updated in the same change (DR-046). (It exists only to feed REQ-08-008 to REQ-08-010.) **Amended 2026-10-22, v2 plan (owner default O2):** the skill-flows data (`src/lib/manual/skill-flows.ts`) and the `FlowGraph` component are removed from the app, and no v2 page reads or renders them.
- **REQ-08-013** — The hand-authored Tutorial / Guías / Conceptos pages SHALL reflect the **current** factory workflow — the v2 build engine (the Build Plan, coarse work orders, repair-before-block, resumable runs) and the recent decision rules — NOT stale earlier content; a change to how the factory is operated SHALL update the affected page in the same change (DR-046; see the root `CLAUDE.md` "Decision log" section). The guide "Elegir modo de construcción" is refreshed in the v2 build: it no longer says "Portfolio → Party" (the Party tab is gone; the mode is chosen in the project's Comandos tab and the Campaña ficha) and no longer states waves of 2/4/8 work orders. (Amended 2026-10-22, v2 plan, change card `design-phase-copy-claude-design-sunset`: the pipeline page and every other Documentación text describing the design stage say that new projects default to hand-written HTML design directions reviewed as private Artifacts, with local files or screenshots as the fallback, and SHALL NOT say that the design phase uses Claude Design.)
- **REQ-08-014** — THE multi-runtime Concept ("Operar desde cualquier agente") SHALL present Codex and Claude Code as having **full read/write permission** over every Pandacorp skill and application, including canonical documentation, work orders and build state (DR-133, owner decision 2026-10-20, superseding the DR-120 read/review-only posture). It SHALL state the shared rules: each runtime uses its own available tools; a missing orchestration or scheduling tool means attended sequential execution, not a permission refusal; human gates, independent verification, atomic ownership, one writer per checkout and safe-point-only cold handoff are preserved; no runtime takes over a live run. It SHALL present the live build executor (`/pandacorp:implement` on Dynamic Workflows with its supervisor) as Claude Code's own, which no other runtime imitates.
- **REQ-08-015** — THE multi-runtime Concept's "how to verify" steps SHALL describe the same checks, with the same rules and gates, for both runtimes. The comparison and diagram surfaces (runtime comparison, two-doors diagram) SHALL label both runtimes as read/write and SHALL NOT promise live build progress, notifications or telemetry for a build run from a runtime that does not provide them.
- **REQ-08-016** — THE multi-runtime Concept SHALL NOT claim certified automation, or enforcement that a runtime lacks, in a real session: it SHALL present a runtime's capability as what is installed and verified there, and a missing capability as attended sequential execution.
- **REQ-08-017** — WHERE a Concept/Guía page describes a project's documentation structure, it SHALL reflect the **feature-centric** layout (DR-049): the shared product layer (`docs/product/`), the per-feature module folders `docs/frds/frd-NN-<slug>/` (`frd.md` + on-demand `fdd.md`/`blueprint.md`/`mocks/`/`work-orders/`), the global `docs/{adr,analytics,reviews,decision-log.md}`, the stable-ID spine (`REQ-NN-MMM → AC-NN-MMM.K → CMP/IF-NN → WO-NN-MMM`) and the source-of-truth hierarchy `FRD > FDD > design-tokens > blueprint > work order` — NOT the old by-type layout (a flat `docs/frds/`, a global `docs/blueprint.md`, a global `docs/work-orders/`). The derived catalogs pick up DR-049 and the rewritten `structure.md` standard automatically.
- **REQ-08-018** — WHERE an authored page has a bespoke renderer (`src/app/manual/manualPages.tsx`), that renderer SHALL be the page's ONLY source of content (DR-115: one writer per fact); its `content/manual/<group>/<slug>.md` SHALL be an **index stub** (frontmatter `title`/`group`/`order` for the page index + the H1 + a one-line summary + a pointer to the renderer) and SHALL NOT carry prose, headings, lists or tables, because the reader never renders it. A page with no bespoke renderer renders its `.md` body and that body SHALL be real prose. A test SHALL fail if a bespoke page's `.md` carries prose, or if a registered renderer has no `.md` (unreachable page).
- **REQ-08-019** — The **"El equipo"** concept SHALL present one bio card (in the owner's language) for EVERY agent shipped in `plugin/agents/`, grouped by phase and drawn from ONE roster shared with the team diagram; the `drift-finder` card SHALL explain what it does (walks every FRD contract against already-verified code, outside the diff, proposing drift with a probe test) and WHEN it runs (only under `gateEvidence: 'digested'`, which is not the default, or when forced with `--drift-finder on`; it only proposes, the reviewer stays the judge). A test SHALL fail when an agent is added to `plugin/agents/` without a card.

### REQ-08-020 — Home, search and reader (added 2026-10-22, design freeze)

- **AC-08-020.1** — THE Documentación home SHALL offer "Empieza aquí" (the numbered tutorial path, then "Después, entiende lo esencial"), "Guías para el día a día", and an explore-by-type block (Tutorial · Guías · Conceptos · Workflows) whose Referencia entry points to Configuración.
- **AC-08-020.2** — WHEN the owner searches the Documentación, THE system SHALL list the matching pages from every group, each with a text snippet.
- **AC-08-020.3** — THE reader SHALL show breadcrumbs, a "Paso X de Y" progress bar on tutorial pages, previous/next links within the page's group, and a page index to the RIGHT of the page with its own search, folded behind a control below 900 px.

**Worked example (AC-08-020.3).** The owner opens the third page of a six-page tutorial: breadcrumbs "Documentación › Tutorial › <page>", the bar reads "Paso 3 de 6", "Anterior" and "Siguiente" move within the tutorial group, and the index on the right lists the group's pages with a search box. At 375 px the index is folded behind a button.

## Edge cases
- A page address that does not exist → "Esta página no existe" with "La Documentación no tiene una página «no-existe»." and "Ir al inicio de la Documentación" (FRD-25 owns the not-found contract).
- A bespoke page whose `.md` stub carries prose, or a renderer without a stub → a failing test (REQ-08-018).
- An agent added without a bio card → a failing test (REQ-08-019).

## Does NOT include
- The per-skill curated explainer, the interactive flow graph and its cross-navigation (REQ-08-008 to REQ-08-010, REQ-08-012): out of v2, and their data (`skill-flows.ts`) and `FlowGraph` component are removed, not kept dormant (amended 2026-10-22, v2 plan, owner default O2).
- Re-implementing the catalogs: Referencia points to Configuración (FRD-07).

> **Prototype note.** While only the HTML prototype exists (`docs/design/prototype/index.html`), the "Documentación" surface (`manualView`) renders the group index and points Referencia to Configuración; the Next.js app implements the dynamic derivation above by reading the source files directly. See the Mission Control decision-log (2026-06-15).

## Persistent navigation integration — FRD-25 (2026-10-17)

The [cross-cutting URL contract](../frd-25-persistent-navigation/frd.md) supersedes earlier local-only tab/detail state, exact-root navigation matching and display-name-derived project addresses in this document. Meaningful selections/filters are URL-owned, legacy links retain their resource and presentation context, and reload/Back/Forward restore that location. Existing visual design, data-read boundaries, action gates and machine-status history remain unchanged. Implementation scope and evidence live in FRD-25's blueprint/work-order record and delivery note.


## Visual close-out clarification (2026-10-31, lane O)

The home category cards and reader index retain the approved category icons. Reference links keep their canonical Configuration destinations; the phone index remains disclosed above the reader and Escape returns focus to its trigger. Counts derive from the currently authored pages.
