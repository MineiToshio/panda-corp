# FRD-17 — Proposals inbox (self-learning gate + self-suggestion)

> **Design freeze amendment (2026-10-22, S2.2).** The approved Órbita prototype is the oracle. The interface calls this surface "Propuestas" (a sidebar destination), organized as an ordered to-do with the tabs Resumen (default), Memoria and Backlog (FRD-22). The proposal streams are replaced by the to-do model (contradiction C-17 of `docs/design/freeze/reconciliation.md`). Items the owner left out of v2 (decision 2026-10-21) stay in place, prefixed **Out of v2**. Prototype-drawn behaviours are REQ-17-011 (AC-17-011.1 to AC-17-011.4).

The owner-facing surface of the factory's self-learning loop (DR-047) and the place where Mission Control **suggests improvements on its own**. It aggregates, for the owner to approve or dismiss: candidate **lessons** harvested into `factory/memory/`, **promotion** proposals (a recurring lesson → a standard/rule/skill), prune proposals, and **self-suggestions** Mission Control computes locally from the data it already reads (bottlenecks, velocity, unused capability, a project that merits a launch review). Derived from [docs/proposals/09-self-learning-factory.md](../../../../docs/proposals/09-self-learning-factory.md). Read-only: it shows the proposal and the command; it never harvests, promotes, prunes or runs a skill — the owner does, through the skills.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

- **Loop v2 fields & signals (REQ-17-009, 2026-07-02 — factory proposal 23 / plugin v9.49.0).** The lesson reader SHALL parse the loop-v2 schema fields — `trigger:` ("use this when …", the retrieval condition) and `applied_in:` (the projects whose artifacts cited the lesson, script-maintained) — fail-soft per lesson (a pre-v2 lesson yields `trigger: ""` / `appliedIn: []`, never a skip). WHERE a lesson has applied-in projects, THE lesson/promotion detail SHALL show them as "Aplicada en: <projects>" (as the captured lesson dialog draws it, for example "Aplicada en: funko-tracker, pandasaga"). (Amended 2026-10-22, v2 reconciliation) WHERE the lesson has a retrieval trigger, THE lesson/promotion dialog SHALL show it in the drawn box (a blue box with a target icon, state `proposals-lesson-dialog`), without a label. **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** only the labelled "Úsala cuando" row. `memoryHealth()` SHALL additionally report `lastSweepAt` (the ISO content of the daily sweep marker `factory/memory/_last-sweep`; null honest when absent/unparseable — shown as the REAL timestamp in "Última revisión automática: <fecha> · al día" instead of the "(aprox.)" mtime proxy) and `harvestOrphans` (portfolio projects whose `.pandacorp/status.yaml` says `phase: release` with NO `last_harvest:` stamp — a build that closed without harvesting; honest empty otherwise) (AC-17-006.2/.3); **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** surfacing `harvestOrphans` as a danger banner naming each project + the `/pandacorp:memory harvest` command. The raw-notes nudge threshold SHALL match the loop-v2 sweep threshold (20).

- **REQ-17-012** — Mission Control SHALL show **"Propuestas"** as a sidebar destination at guild level, with a badge counting the actions that need the owner (AC-17-011.2). **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** the per-project chip in the portfolio rail (the rail is gone) and the "Crónica del gremio" alias.
- **REQ-17-013** — The inbox SHALL aggregate four proposal kinds: (1) **candidate lessons** awaiting corroboration or the owner (`factory/memory/` entries with `status: candidate`; "Candidatas"); (2) **promotions** — a lesson active across ≥2 projects proposed for a standard / decision rule / skill (DR-047 high-risk tier; "Esperan tu decisión"); (3) **prune** ("Para podar") — lessons proposed for deprecation/reconciliation by `/pandacorp:memory review` (v2 draws a "Para podar" card with its count, its prunable lessons and the group command `/pandacorp:memory review` ONLY WHEN at least one lesson is prunable; WHEN none is, no card is drawn and the health line of "Cómo avanza una lección" reads "Ninguna lección obsoleta."); (4) **self-suggestions** computed by Mission Control ("Señales automáticas"). **Promotions are surfaced ONLY via the durable promotions queue (REQ-17-006), NOT also as a dismissable stream** — the earlier redundant dismissable promotion stream was removed (DR-103 cleanup, 2026-07-01): a promotion is an "approve me" item (the queue carries its `/pandacorp:learn` command + target + rationale + evidence + high-risk badge), not noise to dismiss. So the dismissable streams are candidate-lessons, prune and self-suggestions; promotions live in the queue.
- **REQ-17-014** — Self-suggestion **`unused-capability`** SHALL be emitted ONLY for **invocable skills** (`/pandacorp:<skill>`), never for agents or non-invocable capabilities (a self-suggestion's whole value is a runnable command; the drawn signal reads "Skills sin uso registrado"). **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** the **dismiss affordance**, a subtle icon-only ✕ in each card's top-right corner (not a text+border pill), keyboard-reachable with an `aria-label` (DR-103 cleanup, 2026-07-01).
- **REQ-17-015** — **Lesson detail (click a lesson card → formatted detail).** Each **lesson** card (candidate + prune) SHALL be an accessible control (a `<button>`, keyboard-operable, `aria-label` naming the lesson) that opens a **detail modal** rendering the lesson's full markdown **body** (`Lesson.body`) with the shared `Markdown` primitive — the same formatted rendering used for the FRD/backlog detail (real headings for Situation / Lesson / Apply next time / Why it matters), plus a metadata strip (type·domain, status, eval-gate, promotion, source, links). The detail opens in the shared `Modal` (focus-trap + Escape). Self-suggestions (which have no markdown body) are NOT clickable. Reuses `core/Modal` + `core/Markdown` (DR-057). Detail-view parity added 2026-07-01, mirroring FRD-22 REQ-22-005.
- **Promotion detail (click a promotion card → formatted detail, AC-17-006.7).** Each **promotions-queue** entry (proposed + rejected) SHALL likewise be an accessible control that opens the **same** `LessonDetailModal` (sectioned body + metadata strip). The promotion detail additionally shows the copyable `/pandacorp:learn` command (proposed only) so the owner can approve straight from the modal (`LessonDetail`/`LessonDetailModal` gained an optional `command` prop; the candidate/prune modals omit it). Its card SHALL show a **clean one-line summary** (the lesson's `context`, or the first body line with markdown markers stripped), NOT a raw 300-char markdown excerpt. To keep the module (`components/modules/PromotionsQueue`) free of a route-local import, the queue only emits `onSelectLesson`; the modal state lives in a route-local wrapper (`PromotionsQueuePanel`) — the same split the candidate/prune streams use. This closes the follow-up deferred on 2026-07-01. Reuses `core/Modal` + `SectionedMarkdown` + `CopyButton` (DR-057).
- **REQ-17-016** — **Lesson card title = the `context` field.** A lesson card's title SHALL be the lesson's frontmatter **`context`** (a clean, human-written one-line summary the reader now exposes), NOT a heuristic derived from the body (which produced broken titles like a lone "." — removed). The lesson **detail body** SHALL parse the bold inline labels (`**Situation:**`, `**Lesson:**`, `**Apply next time:**`, `**Why it matters:**`) into **titled, colour-coded sections** (icon + coloured header per part, content via `Markdown`) so the parts are distinguishable instead of one wall of bold-prefixed prose. This sectioning lives in the shared `SectionedMarkdown` primitive (auto-detects `**Label:**` vs `## Heading` sections), reused by the backlog detail (FRD-22 REQ-22-005) so both modals share one look (DR-062); extracted 2026-07-01.
- **REQ-17-017** — **Group command is `/pandacorp:memory review`** (the mode that acts on them — corroborates/activates candidates, proposes pruning obsolete ones), not the bare `/pandacorp:memory`; v2 shows it on the "Para podar" card only, when that card is drawn (the candidates carry no command, "no piden nada"). **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** each stream showing a one-line explainer of what the list is (e.g. how "obsoleta" is determined: a lesson `/pandacorp:memory review` marked `status: deprecated` — never retrieved + old, or contradicted by newer evidence; deprecated, never deleted). (2026-07-01)
- **REQ-17-018** — FOR each proposal, Mission Control SHALL show its **evidence/source** (the `LESSON-NNNN`, the project + capture point, or the metric), the **suggested action**, the **exact command** to act on it — `/pandacorp:memory review`, `/pandacorp:learn …`, `/pandacorp:decide`, or `/pandacorp:review-launch` — ready to copy, and where to run it ("Córrelo en la carpeta de la fábrica"). Mission Control SHALL NOT run it (read-only, like [FRD-15](../frd-15-plugin-out-of-sync-warning/frd.md)/[FRD-16](../frd-16-orphan-project-detection/frd.md)).
- **REQ-17-019** — Mission Control SHALL compute self-suggestions **locally, with no Claude calls** (consistent with [FRD-01](../frd-01-data-reading/frd.md)), from data it already reads: **bottlenecks** (N ideas stuck in one phase, from the board derivation); **velocity** alerts (a phase running far longer than the portfolio norm, from the `~/.claude/dashboard-events.ndjson` tail and `.pandacorp/status.yaml`); **unused capability** (a skill or agent with no recorded usage); **policy friction** (a `requiere_humano: false` decision rule that keeps reappearing in `.pandacorp/inbox/decisions.md`); a **recurring lesson** (≥2 projects in `factory/memory/` sources) proposed for promotion; and a **shipped project** whose age/metrics merit `/pandacorp:review-launch` (DR-043).
- **REQ-17-020** — Mission Control SHALL show a **memory-health panel** for the self-learning loop: the count of **pending raw notes** awaiting refinement (the inbox size — `.pandacorp/run/lessons.md` per project + `factory/memory/_inbox.md`), the count of **candidate lessons** awaiting corroboration/promotion (`status: candidate` in `factory/memory/`), and **when `/pandacorp:memory` last ran** (harvest/review). The drawn panel reads "Notas sin refinar", "Candidatas", "Esperan tu decisión", "Reglas activas" and "Última revisión automática: <fecha> · al día". **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** IF the backlog is large or it has not run in a while, it SHALL nudge with the exact command (`/pandacorp:memory harvest` / `review`) — a staleness reminder, like FRD-15's drift banner. This panel doubles as the on-demand **refine trigger surface**: the owner runs the refinement when the panel says there is something to consolidate, so the cadence is **scheduled sweep + this reminder**, not tied to any single skill (e.g. not only `implement`).
- **REQ-17-021** — The **promotions queue** SHALL be a durable, reviewable list — every `factory/memory/` lesson with **`promotion: proposed`** (set by `/pandacorp:memory review`), showing its target (standard / decision-rule / skill), the rationale, and the evidence. The owner reviews the **full list and decides whenever** (no in-the-moment pressure): **approve** surfaces the `/pandacorp:learn <id>` command that promotes it (→ `promotion: approved`); **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** **reject** marking `promotion: rejected` (the lesson stays, but never becomes a rule). The target, rationale, evidence and high-risk badge show in the lesson dialog. The promotion state lives on the lesson (single source of truth); this page and `/pandacorp:memory status` are both views of it. Mission Control never promotes — it shows the list + the command (read-only, like FRD-15/16).
- **REQ-17-022** — Candidate lessons SHALL be visually distinct from `active` ones (a collapsed "Candidatas" card, with a "Nueva" chip on fresh two-project lessons), and the eval-gate state SHALL be shown ("se confirman solas cuando vuelven a aparecer en otro proyecto"), so the owner sees why something is auto-active vs pending (DR-047).
- **REQ-17-023** — Proposals SHALL be **honest** (White-Hat, [FRD-09](../frd-09-gamification/frd.md)): no false urgency, no nagging, no streaks; framed in the guild theme (the `librarian` is the guild's cronista), never as pressure. **Out of v2 (owner decision 2026-10-21):** a proposal being a suggestion the owner can dismiss, and dismissing it being remembered.
- **REQ-17-024** — High-risk proposals (promote to a MUST standard, create/edit a skill/agent, change a DR, delete) SHALL only ever be **displayed**; approval routes to the owner running the relevant skill — Mission Control never applies them itself (DR-047 human gate).

### REQ-17-010 — Page long proposal streams with a "Mostrar más" bar
- **AC-17-010.1** — WHERE a proposal stream has **more cards than its page size** (Candidatas 10, Esperan tu decisión 8; Señales automáticas show all), the shared stream component (`CMP-17-stream`) SHALL render only the first page and a full-width bar **"Mostrar N más · quedan M"**; the remaining cards SHALL NOT be in the rendered list until revealed. (Amended 2026-10-22: it replaced the "ver más" toggle with threshold 6.)
- **AC-17-010.2** — WHEN the owner activates the bar, the next page of the **already-loaded** cards SHALL appear **in-place** (no additional fetch or navigation); there is no collapse back ("ver menos" is not drawn).
- **AC-17-010.3** — WHERE a stream has **no more cards than its page size**, **no bar** SHALL be shown and every card SHALL render (unchanged behavior).
- **AC-17-010.4** — The paging SHALL live in the **single shared** "Mostrar más" component the whole app uses (memory, backlog, rules, trophies, transitions; Órbita iteration 28) and apply **uniformly to every stream kind** it serves — one code path, not a per-kind fork (DR-057). Existing detail-dialog / group-level-command behavior SHALL be unchanged; the bar is a semantic `<button>`, keyboard-operable, Spanish copy, state not by color alone (FRD-13). Added 2026-07-05 (drains change `mc-proposal-stream-show-more`, DR-069).

### REQ-17-001 — Group-level command + lesson grouping
- **AC-17-001.1** — WHERE a proposal group shares a single activating command (e.g. candidate lessons and prunable/obsolete lessons both act through `/pandacorp:memory`), the command SHALL be shown **once at the group level**, under the group title — NOT as a per-item copy button.
- **AC-17-001.2** — A proposal item SHALL show its own per-item command **only when** its command genuinely differs from the rest of its group (e.g. each self-suggestion routes to a different skill); otherwise the item is title + evidence only and defers to the group-level command.
- **AC-17-001.3** — The Memoria tab SHALL list **"Esperan tu decisión"** (the promotions queue) first, then the collapsed **"Candidatas"**, then **"Para podar"** (the lesson-oriented groups stay adjacent; amended 2026-10-22), the last drawn ONLY WHEN at least one lesson is prunable (otherwise the health line reads "Ninguna lección obsoleta.").

### REQ-17-011 — Resumen to-do, navigation badge, automatic signals and the lesson flow (added 2026-10-22, design freeze)

- **AC-17-011.1** — THE Propuestas page SHALL open on the tab Resumen ("Qué te toca, en orden"): an ordered list of only the actions that need the owner, each with its count, its reason, its command, where to run it and a link to the detail; memory upkeep SHALL read "se revisa sola" while the daily routine is current.
- **AC-17-011.2** — THE Propuestas navigation badge SHALL count the owner's actions, not the proposal items.
- **AC-17-011.3** — THE "Señales automáticas" SHALL be grouped by kind, and a signal that flags most of its population (such as the unused-skills signal) SHALL be marked unreliable.
- **AC-17-011.4** — THE Memoria tab SHALL explain a lesson's four steps and who moves each one, SHALL list the lessons awaiting the owner ("Esperan tu decisión") first, and SHALL keep the candidates collapsed under "no piden nada".

**Worked example (AC-17-011.2).** The factory has 42 automatic signals, 16 promotions awaiting the owner and 24 candidate lessons. They group into 4 actions that need the owner, so the badge reads 4, not 82.

## Non-goals
- Mission Control does NOT harvest, promote, prune, or run any skill. It never edits `factory/memory/`, `factory/standards/`, `factory/decisions/registry.yaml` or `plugin/`. The loop's harvest/apply/prune live in `/pandacorp:memory` + `/pandacorp:learn` + the owner.
- No auto-acceptance of high-risk self-modification (DR-047). No Claude calls — suggestions are derived from existing files/events, not generated on the fly.

## Relationship
It extends [FRD-14](../frd-14-snapshot-and-feedback/frd.md) (the chips/feedback channels) with the proposals stream, consumes the event tail of [FRD-12](../frd-12-observability-dataviz/frd.md), and routes promotions into `/pandacorp:learn` (DR-047). The catalog of lessons and rules is surfaced read-only in the Documentación ([FRD-08](../frd-08-documentation/frd.md)), which gains a "Self-learning loop" Concept page (DR-046). The state is written by the skills and the `librarian`, never by Mission Control.

## Persistent navigation integration — FRD-25 (2026-10-17)

The [cross-cutting URL contract](../frd-25-persistent-navigation/frd.md) supersedes earlier local-only tab/detail state, exact-root navigation matching and display-name-derived project addresses in this document. Meaningful selections/filters are URL-owned, legacy links retain their resource and presentation context, and reload/Back/Forward restore that location. Existing visual design, data-read boundaries, action gates and machine-status history remain unchanged. Implementation scope and evidence live in FRD-25's blueprint/work-order record and delivery note.
