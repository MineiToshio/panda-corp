**Situation:** a turn produced a long, detailed artifact (a report/analysis). The owner's reaction was that
it was too much to read and asked explicitly for "bullets súper puntuales, reporte gerencial."

**Lesson:** CONV-11/DR-110 already asks for concise, scannable, high-level-by-default replies — this
sharpens it into a concrete delivery ORDER whenever a long artifact is the deliverable: a short managerial
executive summary goes in the CHAT MESSAGE first (the few bullets that actually matter — conclusion,
decision, risk), and the full artifact is the appendix a reader opens only if they want the detail. Never
lead with "here is the artifact" and let the owner discover the summary is missing or buried inside it.

**Apply next time:** whenever the main output of a turn is a long document/report/analysis, write the
chat reply as: (1) a handful of punctual, managerial bullets stating the conclusion/decision/risk first,
(2) then the artifact link/pointer as the appendix. Don't wait for the owner to ask for the short version
a second time.
