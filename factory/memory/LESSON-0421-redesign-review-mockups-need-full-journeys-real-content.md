**Situation:** for a redesign of an already-shipped internal dashboard, a single representative screen with
placeholder content was not enough for the owner to judge the direction — he needed to walk complete
sections of the app (board plus a project's full pipeline: spec, architecture, documents, work orders) with
real content standing in each one.

**Lesson:** evaluating a REDESIGN is a different judgment than evaluating a new style direction in the
abstract — the owner is implicitly comparing the new look against the real, lived experience of the current
app across its actual sections. A single screen, or a flow populated with lorem/placeholder boxes, lets him
judge only the chrome in isolation; it cannot show whether the redesign still works end-to-end once real
content (varying lengths, real states, real density) flows through it.

**Apply next time:** for a redesign (as opposed to a greenfield design-exploration pass), build complete
recorridos — every section the redesign actually touches, each populated with real or representative
content pulled from the live product — rather than one polished screen with placeholder text. This is the
same "real content over placeholders" principle as LESSON-0304/LESSON-0317, applied to flow SCOPE
(complete journeys, not an isolated screen) rather than to image assets or data volume.
