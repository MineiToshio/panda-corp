**Situation:** a decision about how to source demo images had two paths: a slower path with upfront
licensing clearance, and a faster, pragmatic path (reuse credited internet images with a link to origin
and comply with any takedown request) that carries real but reversible legal exposure. For a product with
no validated users yet, the owner chose the faster path and explicitly asked that legal considerations be
framed as a flagged risk with a stated revisit trigger (before monetizing), not as a blocker preventing
the MVP from shipping.

**Lesson:** for an unproven MVP (no users validated the demand yet), this owner's standing preference is
to accept a pragmatic, REVERSIBLE risk over paying the upfront cost of full legal/compliance rigor, as
long as the exposure has a clear mitigation (credit, link, honor takedown) and a named point where it
must be revisited (before monetizing, before scaling). Presenting a legal point as an absolute blocker on
an unproven MVP, rather than a risk note with a revisit trigger, mismatches this owner's actual risk
tolerance and can stall validation work that doesn't need to stall.

**Apply next time:** when a legal/compliance question arises for a pre-validation MVP and a pragmatic,
reversible mitigation exists, present it as a short risk note (what the exposure is, the mitigation, the
revisit trigger) rather than treating it as a blocking requirement — reserve hard blocking for risks that
are NOT reversible (e.g. anything that can't simply be taken down/undone on request) or that cross into
monetization/scale, where the posture should shift.
