## Corrections that close the remaining gaps

1. **Make D one coherent choice.** New conflict introduced by the faster variant. EN 258 and ES 41/43 let the owner accept a temporary reversible UI failure before the ordinary pilot finishes. EN 440 imports *all* Phase 3 constraints, but EN 437 requires Phase 2 results and limits the project to pre-launch. EN 333–334 and 438 exclude an early Mission Control exception. EN 323/340 promise background execution/receipts even though EN 258 describes the risk while those guarantees are absent. ES 69–70 similarly read as unconditional. There is no second recommendation: EN 35/377/379 describe the same default and ES 43 names B alone. Preserve that choice; label §§11.3 and 15 and ES 66–70 as the **default** wherever D differs.

   **Minimal decision text, EN 355–359/373, 399, 438–440:** “Under B, Phase 2 results and owner approval admit the Phase 3 pilot. Phase 3 results inform expansion. Under D, the owner may name an isolated pre-launch project or expressly reopen 4A for a bounded Mission Control change and start during Phase 1. This waives the ordinary evidence timetable only for the named scope. Before first use, define a distinct partial-proof record, its eligible changes and the route back to full proof. A partial result cannot issue a full certificate. Record any background detection guarantee still absent as an accepted risk. For Mission Control this is an explicit exception to DR-165’s 4A decision. Launched projects retain full proof.” Qualify “not first”, “its current suite”, “any future deferral”, and the automatic receipt promise consistently. EN 451 should say that only the pre-launch path needs a named pre-launch project.

   **ES 41, 64–70:** “Las fases siguientes describen el plan recomendado, B. Si eliges D, durante la fase 1 puedes nombrar un proyecto aislado aún no lanzado o autorizar por separado un cambio reversible y acotado de Mission Control. Se mantienen la comprobación de tipos, la compilación, una prueba del cambio y una interacción en la versión de producción. Una comprobación parcial se registrará como tal. Los cambios de datos, permisos, funciones compartidas o efecto incierto siguen el control completo.” The memo may instead elect to require receipts before first use, but then both risk cards must promise only what those receipts establish and state explicitly what D actually accelerates.
