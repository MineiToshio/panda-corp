## 6. Losses, decision triggers and migration

**What goes away on B.** The owner gets less per-unit timeline detail, less speculative scheduler parallelism, less unattended relaying and less independent implementation specialization in the default loop. A persistent author can normalize its own assumptions, overfit tests, hit context limits, or get trapped in one provider's quota. A coarse handoff can lose thought context even when it preserves work. A reviewed release can still have defects beyond the executed behavior. Capture checkpoints and independent executable evidence, then measure where the trade pays. [Prompt audit risks](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-sergio--claude-spares-spare-Kis6jz/486d4b23-cf7f-44e5-b370-390dd1a1a812/scratchpad/audit/prompts.md:234) · [Mission Control tracking](</Users/Shared/Proyectos/panda-corp/mission-control/src/lib/build-track/build-track.ts:2>).

**Remaining failure modes.** Model and provider outages, true capacity limits, missing specs, dependencies between parallel changes, race conditions in shared resources, busy main, rebase conflicts, legitimate owner decisions, and the time needed to build a production artifact and execute the full queue suite can all remain. Real production configuration and data size can still differ from fixtures. In phase one, **4 MEASURED** of **26 MEASURED** owned/repair queue reds demonstrated bad public or operator outcomes and **one MEASURED** blocked an unbuildable candidate. Earlier preventive yield and escape rates are unknown. Review plus a clean app deploy cannot undo destructive actions in live data. **What changes the recommendation.** Choose A for ordinary work if common patches and lean prose reproduce the gains at equally reliable final proof, or if B's apparent speed is erased by review, setup, recovery or weaker first-pass quality. Keep A for high-risk/wide work where independent test authoring, hardening or scoped scheduling demonstrably earns its cost. Choose C for the classes where it matches B on recovery, enforcement, docs/state, owner burden and final proof while consistently taking less time. Lighter launched proof is a separate phase-one decision requiring its own evidence and authority.
