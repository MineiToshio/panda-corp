# Why vanilla was faster, and a lean design for Pandacorp implement

## Decision

Shadow-test one persistent builder per cohesive, pre-scoped outcome, using native Claude Code or Codex sessions inside engine-owned isolation. Feed it the relevant work orders, acceptance criteria, boundaries and proof commands. Let it choose the implementation sequence and repair in context. Keep an independent check at the candidate boundary, with the current authoritative rebased release proof and owner authorization intact. Retain the current path for risk-floor cases until equivalent coverage is demonstrated, and include the existing reproduction requirement for bug cards in the lean design. This applies the owner's Option B to the implementation loop. [Proposal 44, lines 33–42, 263–279, 280–338](</Users/Shared/Proyectos/panda-corp/docs/proposals/44-verification-strategy.md:33>).

The internal speed advantage is real on the measured tasks. A general error advantage is unsupported. The vanilla agent passed the saturated small oracle and escaped some factory-builder defects. It also shipped a typography defect in one judged run and missed keyboard-focus behavior on one medium run. The factory caught issues its oracle missed and generated its own failure classes. The experiment compares workflows with different duties and finish lines. It cannot isolate prose length, model routing, fragmentation, test effort or review as a single cause.

### Why the speed gap is plausible and where it comes from

1. **The engine produced considerably more and waited for additional outcomes.** Proposal 41 attributed **MEASURED, archived** 71.5% of summed builder time across 20 transcripts to model generation/turn latency. The builder produced 4.2–4.8× the output tokens of vanilla, with 8–10× unit-test volume, about 6× browser-spec volume and 1.3–1.7× app code. Across its instrumented runs, **MEASURED** orientation took 0.5–2.4 minutes per builder, summing to 23.5 minutes, and there were 19 AC citation refusals and 6 reruns solely to recover an exit status hidden by a pipeline. Of 6 self-check reds, the analysis classified 2 as real and 4 as environment/partial build. Output volume, wall time and summed agent time are different measures. A lean launch string pays off if it changes the trajectory or avoids restart. Merely shaving prompt tokens cannot explain these numbers. [Proposal 41 lines 8–17, 24–36](</Users/Shared/Proyectos/panda-corp/docs/proposals/41-builder-speed.md:8>).
2. **Fragmentation introduced real work.** In the original A-1 audit, the UI builder spent **MEASURED, archived** about 13 minutes looking into a Playwright hydration race. The gate spent 25.4 minutes and rediscovered the known class merge issue and unused exports. That builder had a credible fix but no write authority for the seam. B-2 fixed it within its own scope. Vanilla lacked a pre-hydration protection present in the engine implementation, whose product importance was unverified by the oracle. Keeping related UI, validation and integration under one owner permits repair where diagnosis happens. It also concentrates author bias. MC benchmark transcript `:676`.
3. **Some failures arise at boundaries the direct session has fewer reasons to cross.** BL-0220 records **MEASURED, archived** 160/160 original targeted workflow agents inheriting Mission Control instructions even though the brief told them to change directories. That affects this benchmark's fairness. The historical patch warned when the session actually started outside the target checkout. Today's Codex launcher explicitly supplies the unit directory, prompt and schema. A warning or shell `cd` alone is no assurance that policy came from the right project. [BL-0220 lines 18–35](</Users/Shared/Proyectos/panda-corp/factory/backlog/BL-0220-workflow-agents-get-launcher-claude-md.md:18>), `plugin/runtime/build/workers.mjs:119–148`.
4. **The later Funko case separates test execution from test workflow.** Proposal 44's incident has **MEASURED** 89 min 37 s from approval to engine stop across two runs with no feature implementer dispatch, and **MEASURED** 12.868 s across timed stages of the two failed docs G1 invocations. ## Proposed prompts and flow

### Entry and engine responsibilities

For a fully scoped card, validate the selected scope and the committed design source, then start work. For unclear product intent, create a bounded scope delta and obtain only the missing material decision. For a routine bug, include the repro, expected behavior and proof that the focused regression detects the defect in the baseline, as required by `plugin/skills/bug/SKILL.md:17–19`. The current policy also schedules a tests unit for bug cards (`plugin/runtime/model-tiers.json:43–48`). Any pilot that moves that test to the persistent builder must record the author-independence tradeoff and keep the current protected oracle for high-risk bugs. A report of failing infrastructure belongs to runtime recovery. Think in terms of an issue's lifecycle, with multiple isolated issue sessions only when their write sets, mutable services and verification resources are genuinely independent.

### Independent reviewer prompt text

```text
Review candidate {{tree and base}} for the acceptance criteria at {{anchors}} and the explicit risks {{risk signals}}. You have a fresh context and access to the pinned diff and independent reproduction commands {{commands}}. The author summary is a pointer to evidence. Judge the behavior using the actual candidate and the approved requirements.

Look for missing behavior, regressions at affected seams, and violations of the recorded scope or protected expectations. For UI work, check the relevant normal and keyboard paths against the approved design and acceptance criteria. For each material finding, give the criterion or invariant, file or surface, reproduction, and expected versus observed result. A focused failing probe is welcome when it needs to survive repair. Report unrelated or cosmetic observations as advisory; leave the candidate unchanged.

Return pass, needs repair, or blocked with findings and any evidence gap that affects the verdict. Do not certify or land the candidate. The runner sends actionable findings to the original builder.
```

For machine integration, use a single small semantic result, for example candidate status, summary and optional blocker or findings. Stamp attempt identity and pinned revision in the runner. A disclosed failure to produce valid structured text is distinct from an invalid candidate. Structured response failure prompts schema/transport correction or a compact result retry, while preserving code and logs. A review probe can point at an external fixture or a protected test file with a red proof. A pass requires adequate observed evidence. A material evidence gap is blocked. Paths, hashes and command receipts should come from tools. This keeps falsifiable reports and removes model-authored bookkeeping.

### Losses and rules for keeping or rejecting the pilot

The engine currently offers per-WO attempt states and resumable branches, per-FRD evidence and per-unit allocation or resumption after quota pauses (`plugin/skills/implement/references/{engine-v2,drive,rescue}.md`). A persistent builder concentrates ownership and model bias. It can miss independent findings, overfit its own tests, widen a patch, or accumulate context and get stuck. Coarse checkpoints may reduce dashboard detail and resume precision. Concurrent independent FRDs can have better throughput than a single writer when they actually share little. An independent checkpoint, automatic checkpoints and immutable evidence mitigate but do not prove equivalence. Keep deterministic risk detection, direct vs reviewed status distinction, owner order, a runnable outcome checklist and optional exceptional partitioning. Require a concrete reason before splitting work: high-risk independent oracle, substantial read-only exploration, clearly disjoint build slices or demonstrable context exhaustion. Use shared artifacts (pinned intent/ACs, diff, exact error/command, previous attempts and assumptions) as handoff content. Keep costly retry count in the engine, with no no-progress loop on identical evidence.

**Experiment, proposed only.** Freeze same base, spec, acceptance and public/hidden fixtures; reserve a private buffer for the run outputs. Randomize order within task and run in equally provisioned clean environments. Include warm/cold bootstrap in separate strata, mock providers for production-like checks, and an unchanged same-arm rerun to estimate natural variance. Retain failures, early stops, quota interruptions and recoveries as outcomes. Let the headless arm commit to its isolated branch where its prompt asks it to. Apply the same evaluation and final queue-equivalent proof to every candidate in shadow, with no production side effect.

- Compare current engine, current engine with lean role text but same topology, persistent builder with rich task facts and lean flow, and the historical short vanilla brief as a reference. The current-with-lean-text arm estimates a prompt-layer contribution. A common final proof and a separately recorded candidate-ready clock expose review/landing tax. Record model, effort, injected project policies, retries and available tools to make nominal matches real.
- Use planned greenfield, a real brownfield bug, a design/UI focus case, a coupled API/UI change and a limited high-risk negative control. Use at least two runtimes/models in separate strata. A first pass of **ESTIMATED pilot design** 8–12 matched issues with repeated runs on discordant tasks can screen large effects. It cannot estimate rare release regressions reliably. Expand the shadow cohort by risk class before any production rollout.
- Measure to first substantive write, candidate-ready, rebased-certified and ready-to-publish, with owner hold and publication timing reported separately. Capture token/cache usage and wall vs summed worker hours, reorientation, agent count, duplicate checks, retry causes, owner touches, patch/test size, existing failures, independent severity-tagged findings, real regressions, scope/protected-test violations and repair rounds. Report median and tail latency, paired deltas and uncertainty, retaining a denominator for incomplete runs. Define “first pass” as acceptance against independent proof before external review correction, not absence of in-context iterations.
- The hidden assessment should discriminate. Preserve the medium keyboard-focus cases, consumer-contract/data integrity cases, a production-runtime smoke and blind maintainability review. Plant representative defects in frozen candidate copies for reviewer recall and false-block rates. Use defect reproductions and scores as separate measures. Keep exact authorship metadata hidden from adjudicators. Judge task implementation and scope without rewarding raw test volume. Track which verifier first observed each defect, including false blocks.
- **ESTIMATED pilot bars for agreement before running:** seek at least 30% lower median paired time to candidate and 20% lower time to identical proof without worse tail stalls or higher owner interventions. Flag more than a 5 percentage-point decline on material acceptance pass rate for investigation. Any new critical escape, settings/owner-order bypass, protected-oracle mutation or unauthorized scope change kills that lane until fixed and replayed. Repeated losses on maintainability, regressions, proof completeness or recovery are enough to retain the existing stronger path even with speedup. Zero critical escapes in a small sample cannot certify safety. Publish counts and confidence bounds instead of declaring equivalence on a saturated oracle.
- If the prompt-only arm reproduces the gains, simplify the prompt before replacing the scheduler. If persistent versus vanilla is close, use a persistent-builder arm with identical extra task facts supplied separately to test context versus orchestration, and standardize model effort before interpreting a remaining gap. If the persistent arm wins on candidate clock and not time to equal proof, the dominant operational problem is in independent verification or landing. If the vanilla baseline wins after equivalent proof and independent quality checks, retire more of the builder orchestration. If the richer pipeline finds materially more defects at acceptable cost, reserve it for the cases where the evidence shows that advantage.
